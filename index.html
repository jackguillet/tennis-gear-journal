    How it works

    A tapped string bed rings at one main pitch, usually between 400 and 700 Hz. Tension rises with the square of that pitch. The app finds the pitch, then works out tension from your head size and the weight per metre of your string (its gauge and material).

    The result is the tension of the whole string bed, which is lower than the tension set on the stringing machine and falls fastest in the first day. Without calibration the number is an estimate, often within 10%. The trend for one racket is reliable either way: use it to see when to restring.
